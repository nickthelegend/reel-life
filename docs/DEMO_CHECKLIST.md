# Demo recording checklist

Target: ~2 minutes. Record the Lens Studio preview (or the device capture button)
while you run the flow.

## Pre-stage before you hit record

Snap3D takes minutes per part and you have 6–7 parts. Do not generate live on
camera unless you cut away.

- [ ] Generate the hero character once, well before recording, and leave the Lens
      running. Have a second character generated as a backup.
- [ ] Record one throwaway take and delete it, so the timeline UI is warm.
- [ ] Confirm all four music moods are imported with correct BPMs (SETUP.md §5) —
      the mood-switch re-grid is one of your best 10 seconds.
- [ ] Clear the table. The puppet is ~20 cm; a busy surface reads as clutter on
      camera.
- [ ] Set playback speed to 2x before the final Play Reel if your poses are slow —
      a fast performance reads better in a short video.

## Shot list

**1. Speak a character into existence** (~15s)
- [ ] Hold the mic button, say the description clearly on camera
- [ ] Show the per-part progress list ticking over — this is the moment people
      realise it's building an armature, not a statue
- [ ] Cut the wait

**2. Place it on a real table** (~10s)
- [ ] Show the reticle finding the surface
- [ ] Hold still, pinch, puppet lands

**3. Puppeteer 2–3 poses** (~35s) — *the core of the video*
- [ ] Grab a limb with a visible hand, move it, Capture Pose
- [ ] **Turn on onion skin** and pose again, so the ghosts of previous poses are
      visible in frame. Do not skip this — it's the shot that explains the whole
      idea without narration
- [ ] Do one hold-to-record live performance so both capture modes are shown

**4. The timeline editor** (~30s)
- [ ] New Take a couple of times so there are 3 chips
- [ ] Pinch-drag a chip to reorder — hold the drag long enough to read
- [ ] Drag a trim handle in, show the chip's duration label change
- [ ] Tap a caption tab, speak a caption

**5. The beat-lock moment** (~10s)
- [ ] Tap the mood button. Say what happened: the score changed *and* the poses
      re-snapped to the new tempo

**6. Play Reel** (~20s)
- [ ] Hit Play Reel, let it run start to finish uninterrupted
- [ ] Captions visible, foley audible, playhead moving across the timeline
- [ ] Hold on the final pose for a beat before cutting

## Say out loud at least once

- "Every part of this puppet was generated separately so I can pose it."
- "Those ghosts are onion skins — same thing stop-motion animators use."
- "It just snapped my poses to the beat of the track."
- "This plays back live in AR — I'm screen recording it to share it."
